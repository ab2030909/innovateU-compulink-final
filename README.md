# innovateU-compulink-final


CompuLink is a dedicated web-based newsletter platform for the Computer Science Society at the International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI). It provides a space for students, especially those from the Department of Computer Science (DCS), to share blogs, explore emerging technologies, discuss new industry trends, publish insights, and showcase their knowledge. The platform encourages student engagement, knowledge sharing, creativity, and collaboration while connecting the academic community with the rapidly evolving world of technology and computer science.
